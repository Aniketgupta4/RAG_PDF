Security & Privacy

Enterprise Security by Architecture

Designed with cryptographic isolation and zero data retention to ensure enterprise assets remain secure and private.

Zero Public Model Training

Document chunks and vector embeddings are passed strictly as transient inference context. Data is never used to train or retrain public foundational models.

Cryptographic Namespace Segregation

Pinecone Serverless vector partitions isolate personal document embeddings into user namespaces (tenant-${userId}), preventing cross-tenant leakage.

Self-Hostable VPC Ready

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers for Enterprise Architects

How does NexusAI eliminate hallucinations?
Our grounded system prompt constrains Google Gemini 2.5 Flash to synthesize answers strictly within the retrieved document chunks. Every fact must be supported by an inline citation to a specific page number and chunk. If information is absent, the model explicitly declares it rather than conjecturing.
How does department-based access control work?
When an administrator uploads a company document, they tag it with a department clearance (e.g., 'HR', 'Finance', 'IT', or 'All'). During search in the Company Knowledge Base, Pinecone vector queries automatically apply a metadata filter matching the requesting user's department, ensuring users cannot retrieve documents outside their authorized division.
Can individual employees still upload their own documents?
Yes. The platform provides a dual-scope architecture: workers can query the central Company Knowledge Base for official policies, or switch to their Personal Sandbox to upload and analyze private research documents in an isolated vector namespace.
How does the administrator manage roles and departments dynamically?
The Admin Command Center includes a full Taxonomy & Governance console where administrators can dynamically add or delete departments, roles, and document categories without altering source code. These updates instantly propagate across user provisioning forms and document upload selectors.
